About

I am an urban planner graduated from the Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET) and completed an MS in Civil Engineering (Transportation Systems) from North Carolina A&T State University. My work covers transportation policy, sustainable systems, urban transit, travel behavior, equity and accessibility. I use data-driven analysis, policy analysis, spatial analysis, surveys, simulation and operational performance evaluation.

I want to connect interdisciplinary research with practical planning and policy: transportation systems that are efficient, reliable, accessible and responsive to diverse communities. I am especially interested in strategies that encourage active transportation and public transit use while improving mobility, public health and the quality of urban life.

Featured project

Graduate research · North Carolina A&T

Customer service and operational performance at North Carolina license plate agencies

  • Combines LLM-based sentiment analysis with queuing theory to evaluate customer service at North Carolina License Plate Agencies (LPAs).
  • Built an interactive GIS dashboard of spatial accessibility to LPAs, using population-weighted hexagonal zones, road-network travel times and service-area analysis.
  • Processed Google Maps reviews through manual coding and a LLaMA-3.2B model to extract indicators of wait time, efficiency, professionalism, accessibility and transaction outcomes.
  • Built a discrete-event queuing simulation from operational and field data, modeling arrivals, service times, booth utilization and customer loss across scenarios.

Master's thesis: A Data-Driven Framework for Improving Operational Efficiency & Compensation Model of License Plate Agencies in North Carolina.
